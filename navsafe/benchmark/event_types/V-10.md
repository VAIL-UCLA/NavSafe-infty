# V-10 · Unsafe Merge / Entry — review checklist

Two roads converge into one, and a car already holds the lane the ego is
entering. Timed to arrive at the merge point with the ego, so the ego has to
judge the gap.

| | |
|---|---|
| **built by** | `V-10.yaml` — 1 car from the harvested bank, `variety: rotate`. |
| **placement** | `merging_lane_chain`: the same-direction lane whose centreline closes on the ego's route. **The gate and the placement read one search** (`HostProbe.merging_lane`), so the event type cannot qualify on one lane and place the car in another. |
| **behaviour** | `dynamic` -> `idm` at 8 m/s, `arrive_with_ego: true`, **`blind_to_ego: true`**. Two lanes that merge need never cross, so the conflict point is their **closest approach** rather than an intersection. Blind, because an IDM car that treats the merging ego as a leader opens the gap by itself and the event type measures nothing. |

## What a human confirms

- [ ] the two roads genuinely converge — the merge point is **outside** any junction, and the ego's route is on the entering road
- [ ] the mainline car holds its speed as the ego arrives; if it yields, `blind_to_ego` did not take
- [ ] the timing lands: the car is at the merge point within a second or two of the ego, not long before or after
- [ ] merging is achievable without leaving the drivable area

## Applies to every event type

- [ ] the asset renders at all, at the right scale, upright, and sitting on the road
- [ ] an actor the manager is not driving this frame is parked far off-screen,
      not deleted — confirm that parked pose is not visible from another camera
- [ ] the actor is close enough, and early enough, that the policy under test
      actually meets it — a recipe pins a spawn, not a meeting, so only the
      rendered episode can show this
