# C-7 · Head-On — review checklist

A narrow two-way corridor, and one oncoming car inside it. The dominant failure
is **mutual freeze, not collision**.

| | |
|---|---|
| **built by** | `C-7.yaml` — 1 car from the harvested bank, `variety: rotate` for appearance variety across scenarios. |
| **placement** | `opposing_lane_chain`, spawned 45 m ego-forward (a **negative** arc, because that chain's arc grows toward the ego). |
| **behaviour** | `dynamic` -> `idm` at 6 m/s with **`blind_to_ego: true`**. Blind is not a detail: IDM is a safe car-following model, so an oncoming car handed the ego as a leader simply brakes and the corridor never has to be shared. |

## What a human confirms

- [ ] the corridor is genuinely too narrow for two vehicles abreast at the meeting point — that is the event type, not the oncoming car
- [ ] the car holds its speed as the ego approaches (if it lifts off, `blind_to_ego` did not reach the driver)
- [ ] a pullout or wide spot exists **before** the meeting point, so yielding is possible
- [ ] the meeting happens inside the corridor, not before or after it

## Applies to every event type

- [ ] the asset renders at all, at the right scale, upright, and sitting on the road
- [ ] an actor the manager is not driving this frame is parked far off-screen,
      not deleted — confirm that parked pose is not visible from another camera
- [ ] the actor is close enough, and early enough, that the policy under test
      actually meets it — a recipe pins a spawn, not a meeting, so only the
      rendered episode can show this
