# I-3 · General Incident — review checklist

Two vehicles at odd yaw blocking the ego's lane on a wide, quiet road.
Everything is static, so there is no timing to solve.

| | |
|---|---|
| **built by** | `I-3.yaml` — 2 cars from the harvested bank. |
| **placement** | `ego_route`, `arc: auto` over the host's straight stretch, laid out 8 m apart; yaw `+28°` / `−35°` and laterals `−0.6` / `+1.4` m, the way a stopped incident actually sits. |
| **behaviour** | `static` -> `static`. Nothing moves; the test is entirely the ego's. |
| **selection** | `multi_lane` (≥3 same-direction lanes) **and** `light_traffic` (≤6 moving logged vehicles) **and** `straight_stretch`. Both new gates exist for the same reason: a blockage on a single carriageway is a wall, and a queue behind it makes the road decide instead of the ego. |

## What a human confirms

- [ ] the blockage is unmistakably an incident, not two parked cars
- [ ] a legal way past exists **and is visible** from the ego's approach
- [ ] the ego has room to decide — the blockage is not revealed from behind a crest or a bend
- [ ] the two cars read as blocking one lane, not as straddling the whole road

## Applies to every event type

- [ ] the asset renders at all, at the right scale, upright, and sitting on the road
- [ ] an actor the manager is not driving this frame is parked far off-screen,
      not deleted — confirm that parked pose is not visible from another camera
- [ ] the actor is close enough, and early enough, that the policy under test
      actually meets it — a recipe pins a spawn, not a meeting, so only the
      rendered episode can show this
