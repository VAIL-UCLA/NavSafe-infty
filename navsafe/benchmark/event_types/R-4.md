# R-4 · Animal-Involved — review checklist

R-3's template with animals: an unfamiliar mover with no social model, which
will not negotiate.

| | |
|---|---|
| **built by** | `R-4.yaml` — the **whole** `animal` bank, `count: all`, sized per **species** from the registry. |
| **placement** | Same rule as R-3: `ego_route`, `conflict_arc: auto` inside `layout.at: crosswalk_1\|straight`. Spread rather than clustered (33 m span). `start_lateral` / `end_lateral` cross from the left verge to beyond the right kerb; laterals are **+left**, so a right-hand kerb is **negative** and `road_edge_right+1.0` would be a metre **into** the carriageway. |
| **behaviour** | `dart_out` -> `social_force` at 2.2–3.4 m/s, `arrive_offset_s: −1.5` so it crosses ahead of the ego rather than into it. |

## What a human confirms

- [ ] **the species are plausible for this street.** The bank resolves to dog / cow / hippo / horse: the selector filters to `source: assetharvester`, and the eight UrbanVerse animals beside them are not on disk. Run `navsafe assets acquire --event-type R-4` before believing a render.
- [ ] **each animal is the size of its species.** All four are calibrated on HEIGHT against `species_dims:`, so an animal's height is right by construction and its length carries the asset's own proportions. An animal that reads as car-sized in the BEV is an uncalibrated PLY, not a placement bug — the render scales an asset by its OWN extent, and `dims:` cannot stretch it.
- [ ] speed and gait are plausible for that species
- [ ] the animal is visible early enough that a competent policy could respond
- [ ] it does not negotiate — no yielding, no eye contact behaviour
- [ ] the miss distance comes from `end_lateral`, not from timing: a dart-out stops where it ends and stays there

## Applies to every event type

- [ ] the asset renders at all, at the right scale, upright, and sitting on the road
- [ ] an actor the manager is not driving this frame is parked far off-screen,
      not deleted — confirm that parked pose is not visible from another camera
- [ ] the actor is close enough, and early enough, that the policy under test
      actually meets it — a recipe pins a spawn, not a meeting, so only the
      rendered episode can show this
