# V-8 · Illegal U-Turn / Turn — review checklist

**Nothing is inserted.** A logged clip in which the ego performs the manoeuvre
the event type is about; nuPlan tags it, so selection is a tag query, not geometry.

| | |
|---|---|
| **built by** | `V-8.yaml` — `cast: []`. |
| **selection** | `tag` miner over val/test: `starting_u_turn`, `starting_unprotected_cross_turn`, `starting_unprotected_noncross_turn`, sensors required. |

## What a human confirms

- [ ] the manoeuvre in the render is the one the tag claims — a net-heading detector also mistakes parking reversals for U-turns
- [ ] do **not** substitute an ordinary right turn and call it V-8
- [ ] the turn happens inside the episode window, after the hand-off

> **Supply.** Only 2 `starting_u_turn` runs exist in val/test with complete
> sensors, and the single seed in `scenes_500.tsv` (891953217984568c) had no
> reconstruction until it was trained explicitly.

## Applies to every event type

- [ ] the asset renders at all, at the right scale, upright, and sitting on the road
- [ ] an actor the manager is not driving this frame is parked far off-screen,
      not deleted — confirm that parked pose is not visible from another camera
- [ ] the actor is close enough, and early enough, that the policy under test
      actually meets it — a recipe pins a spawn, not a meeting, so only the
      rendered episode can show this
