# R-3 · Micromobility — reviewer checklist

A crowd crossing the ego's road — at the marked crosswalk where the host has
one, mid-block where it does not.

| | |
|---|---|
| **built by** | `R-3.yaml` — the **whole** `person` bank, `count: all`. |
| **placement** | `ego_route`, `conflict_arc: auto` inside `layout.at: crosswalk_1\|straight` — the layout window is **centred** on the host's first crosswalk, so the crowd straddles it, and falls back to the straight stretch on a host without one. Tight: 5 m span, 1 m minimum spacing, because the leaf is about a **crowd**, not six separate one-actor events. Sides alternate (laterals are **+left**). |
| **behaviour** | `dart_out` -> `social_force` at 1.2–1.6 m/s, each held at its spawn until the ego is within its own trigger distance (26/18/22/14/24/16 m). Staggered so they arrive in the road at different times and have to negotiate **each other** — the only place social force is visible. |

## What a human confirms

- [ ] whether this host crossed at a **crosswalk** or **mid-block** — `crosswalk_on_route` reports which, and they are different tests
- [ ] they read as a crowd the ego must negotiate, not as a wall
- [ ] the first of them is visible early enough that a competent policy could respond
- [ ] walking pace, not a sprint: at 2.2–3.2 m/s they were across before the ego arrived and the road was clear again by the time it mattered
- [ ] contact would read as VRU severity, not vehicle severity

> **Assets.** This leaf is named Micromobility but currently builds from the
> plain `person` bank, by decision. Wheelchair / scooter / delivery-robot
> embodiments are a later swap, and only the `assets:` selector changes.
